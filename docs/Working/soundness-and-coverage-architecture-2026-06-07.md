# Soundness-and-Coverage Architecture — witness-checking + catalog-driven coverage for Precept's compile-time guarantee

## Goal

When done, every `Proved` proof obligation — and every obligation-less whole-construct proof verdict (satisfiability / constructability) and every structurally-suppressed obligation — carries a re-checkable structured witness that a small trusted checker independently re-verifies (a hollow `Proved` cannot survive the checker), AND a catalog-driven Roslyn analyzer proves at build time that every fault-prone construct shape the catalogs declare reaches a live obligation-emission site that actually stamps **at every walked position of every walked construct** — demonstrated by: (1) a planted false witness for any discharge path is rejected by the checker, including the cross-unit cancellation hole on the paths where the source unit is recoverable, and a stale guard on all **five** guard-narrowing paths (the relational `FlowNarrowing` cell included); (2) a catalog entry declaring `ProofRequirements` whose emission site is deleted, stubbed, or walked-but-not-stamped fails the build, the procedural obligation-synthesis sites (count/length/presence) are each proven to reach a live stamp, **and the walk recurses into every child `TypedExpression` position of every walked construct (member-call arguments included) and is invoked on every expression-bearing field of every typed construct** (the two structural completeness axes this revision adds); (3) the trusted base (checker + witness data-model + coverage analyzers + the shared trusted leaves) is sized against the corrected surface and reaches 100% mutation kill.

> **The §1.5 obligation-stamping family is closed only at the THREE structural levels the coverage axes reach** (emission-site presence, procedural-synthesizer firing, walk-arm presence-plus-stamp-firing) **plus the two completeness levels this revision adds** (child-position recursion completeness; construct-position entry-point completeness). **Three §1.5 cells remain stated-not-analyzer-closed** and are routed to the empirical net + falsifier register, NOT silently implied closed: interior-arithmetic-overflow completeness (probe a), the event-arg constraint-carrying seam (probe b / O-4), and the empty-`ProofRequirements` blind spot (Decision 5 Claim 3a). The family's coverage is *complete for the levels named and explicitly bounded for the three residual cells* — not "genuinely closed" wholesale.

> **Three stated non-closures gate Lock (see Open questions) — two soundness, one coverage.** *Soundness:* (1) the cross-unit cancellation hole (BUG-030) is **NOT closeable at discharge time on the live anchor paths** as the architecture currently stands: on `PriceTimesQuantity`/`QuantityTimesQuantity` the catalog declares the requirement at *dimension* granularity, and the source unit codes (`kg`, `g`) are projected away by `TryProjectUnitToDimension`/`TryProjectCompoundPrice` (`ProofEngine.Qualifiers.cs:944,914`) *before* the obligation discharges — so by discharge time there is no `UnitCode` to put in a witness. Closing it requires the source unit to be captured upstream (at obligation-stamping time, before projection) — **Open Question O-6**. (2) the relational-narrowing tier requires **O-1**. Until O-1 and O-6 are discharged, the soundness theorem is stated as *not-yet-holding* for narrowing-derived intervals and the cross-unit cell, respectively. *Coverage:* (3) the **guard-condition cell** — guards are `TypedExpression?` that can hold faulting arithmetic yet are never walked for obligations (`CollectObligations` passes no `.Guard` to `WalkExpression`) — is a live entry-point-level §1.5 hole whose disposition (walk guards, or state+enforce a non-faulting-guard restriction) turns on a grammar/intent fork the design cannot settle unilaterally — **Open Question O-8**. All three are surfaced, not silently claimed; the doc correctly cannot Lock with them open. (This round's red-team/completeness-critic fixes — the `FlowNarrowing` staleness path, the `ApplyTrustedRuleFacts` witness, the two-leg dead-end stopgap, and the `TypedMemberAccess.Arguments` recursion fix — are all *closed in the architecture*, not added to this gating list.)

## Scope

- **In scope**:
  1. The **witness format/protocol** (`ProofWitness` DU) that realizes spec §0.6 item 13 (structured proof attribution) as a re-checkable certificate, carried on `ProofObligation`.
  2. The **trusted checker** (`ProofChecker`) — verify-simple re-execution for the trivial strategies, validate-hard named-contributor re-verification for the relational/qualifier/compositional strategies, plus arms for the **obligation-less whole-construct verdicts** (satisfiability/constructability, invariant 6) and the **structurally-suppressed** obligations (invariant 5 / Decision 7) — run always-on as a post-Pass-2 stage. The cross-cutting cross-unit (invariant 4) and staleness (invariant 1) predicates are implemented once and applied family-wide.
  3. The **catalog-driven coverage analyzers** that close the obligation-stamping-completeness family (§1.5) at five structural levels: an **emission-side axis** (every `ProofRequirements`-declaring catalog entry reaches a live emission site); a **procedural-synthesis axis** (every count/length/presence/dynamic synthesizer reaches a live stamp); a **discharge-side / walker-presence axis** (every `ProofRequirementKind` has a discharge arm and every `TypedExpression` shape carrying requirements is walked **and actually stamps**, not merely walked — the stamp-firing assertion); a **child-position recursion-completeness axis** (every walk arm recurses into ALL child `TypedExpression`-typed members of its subtype — the set-difference `{declared child `TypedExpression` fields of subtype X} MINUS {positions arm X recurses into}` must be empty; closes the `TypedMemberAccess.Arguments` gap below); and a **construct-position entry-point axis** (`CollectObligations` invokes `WalkExpression` on every `TypedExpression`-bearing field of every typed construct — including guard conditions if they are decided in-scope to raise obligations). Interior-subexpression overflow completeness is explicitly NOT closed by any of these axes and is routed to the empirical net (Decision 5).
- **Out of scope**:
  - The graph-analyzer checker and type-checker checker (the generalization phases). Their *plumbing convention* is designed-for here (one extended witness DU, catalog-as-oracle); the checkers themselves are built later, proof-engine-first.
  - Any new `.precept` language surface (no new syntax/keyword/type/operator/modifier/construct). This is internal soundness architecture.
  - Deciding the cross-unit qualifier-cancellation *policy* (reject / auto-convert / split). That is deferred language surface. This design only aims to make the current cancellation **checkable** — and surfaces (O-6) that, on the live anchor paths, this requires the source unit to be captured at obligation-stamping time, *before* the Unit→Dimension projection destroys it. Where the source unit is recoverable, the checker rejects factor-dropped cancellation conservatively; where it is not (the dimension-granularity catalog requirements on `PriceTimesQuantity`/`QuantityTimesQuantity`), the cell is stated as not-yet-closed pending O-6.
  - The runtime differential-testing harness (§5.1 of the Case) — blocked on the evaluator (a stub today); noted as a dependency, not built here.
- **Deferred to future**:
  - **Karr / relational-expressiveness extensions** (affine-equality basis, octagon, Farkas certificates). The witness DU reserves an extension slot so the future work extends the DU rather than forking it; no relational-certificate verifier is built now. (Soundness-case F-4.)
  - The **§0.6:256 depth-1 → "one bounded global linear solve" relaxation** the owner authorized (F-4): noted as *enabling* the future relational-certificate work, not designed or implemented here. Spec touch is a forward-pointer only.

## Semantic Rules

This design adds a re-verification layer over existing proof verdicts; it introduces no new proof *obligation kinds* and no new expression forms. The "semantic rules" here are the **witness-checking re-verification rules** — the predicate each checker arm evaluates and the soundness it must preserve.

### Witness-checking protocol

```
Engine (UNTRUSTED) discharges obligation O via strategy S, producing witness W:
    TryX(O) : ProofWitness?      (null = not discharged; non-null = Proved-with-certificate)

Checker (TRUSTED) re-verifies, reading only W, O.Requirement, and the catalogs:
    Check(O.Requirement, W, catalogs) : bool
    Check accepts  ⟺  O.Requirement genuinely holds at O.Site
```

The dispositions stay `{Proved, Unresolved}` (`ProofLedger.cs:94-98`). Only `Proved` carries a non-null `Witness`; `Unresolved` carries none — the soundness asymmetry (`soundness-case §0`: only `Proved` is soundness-critical). **Every `Proved` obligation — including those force-`Proved` by the structural-suppression post-pass — must carry a non-null witness** (see the suppression-pass rewrite in Decision 7); a `Proved` with a null `Witness` is itself a checker rejection (a hollow `Proved` with no certificate is the exact failure the checker exists to catch).

**`Strategy`/`ComputedInterval` — stored field cross-checked against the witness projection (a consistency/anti-drift guard, NOT an independent soundness re-verification).** The witness shape *determines* the canonical `Strategy` tag and `ComputedInterval` value; the checker asserts the stored `Strategy`/`ComputedInterval` on the obligation *agrees with* the value projected from the witness shape, and a disagreement is a checker rejection. **This agreement check catches *drift* (two engine outputs disagreeing), not a *wrong-but-consistent* value** (red-team concern 5): for a narrowing-derived obligation, both the stored `ComputedInterval` and the witness projection trace to the same untrusted `BuildNarrowedIntervals`, so the agreement is self-consistent, not independent. **The soundness of `ComputedInterval` rests entirely on invariant 3 + O-1** (re-derive from bare bounds, or re-verify an all-contributor `FlowNarrowingWitness`) — the agreement check is *not* counted toward the soundness theorem for narrowing-derived intervals. This is a deliberate revision of an earlier "make `Strategy`/`ComputedInterval` pure projections of the witness" framing: that framing is unsound because **at least three live sites set `Strategy` outside any witness-producing `Try*` discharge path** — verified by `grep -n 'Strategy = ProofStrategy' ProofEngine*.cs`, which returns **exactly three direct-write sites**: (i) the structural-suppression post-pass `IncorporateForwardingFacts` at `ProofEngine.cs:1181` (unreachable-FromState branch) and (ii) `ProofEngine.cs:1194` (dead-end-to-dead-end branch), both assigning `Strategy = ProofStrategy.Literal` via `with`; and (iii) the trusted-rule-fact post-pass `ApplyTrustedRuleFacts` at `ProofEngine.Composition.cs:109`, assigning `Strategy = ProofStrategy.CompositionalConstraint` via `with` on a still-`Unresolved` obligation, through a *different* code path (`CollectTrustedNumericFacts`/`ResolveNumericSignSet`) than the dispatcher-invoked `TryCompositionalConstraintProof`. Under a pure-projection model every one of these three would become a latent drift bug. Keeping `Strategy` a stored field and having the checker *assert agreement* converts that drift into a caught rejection. **All three direct-write sites must be rewired to construct a witness** (Decision 7 rewires the two suppression writes to `SuppressedByStructureWitness`; the discharge-side-changes section below rewires `ApplyTrustedRuleFacts` to construct a `CompositionalConstraintWitness`), so each stored `Strategy` projection becomes well-defined. The plan re-runs this grep to confirm no fourth site is introduced; the prior "the suppression pass is the known counterexample" framing under-counted (it missed `ApplyTrustedRuleFacts`) and is corrected here.

### Re-verification idioms (two tiers — Verasco verify-simple/validate-hard)

**Tier A — re-execution (verify-simple).** The witness carries the data the engine computed; the checker re-runs the trivial arithmetic, trusting nothing about *how* the engine got it. Shares one arithmetic core (`IntervalContains` band-check). Five strategies: Literal, IntervalContainment, LengthContainment, CountContainment, CollectionGrowth.

```
LiteralWitness(v, cmp, t):        accept ⟺ (v cmp t) re-evaluated true
IntervalContainmentWitness(c,lo,hi): accept ⟺ c.Lo ≥ lo ∧ c.Hi ≤ hi
CountContainmentWitness(lo,hi,min,max): accept ⟺ lo ≥ min ∧ hi ≤ max
```

**Tier B — named-contributor re-verification (validate-hard).** The witness NAMES the contributing facts; the checker re-verifies the named facts hold against the catalogs/`SemanticIndex` and that they imply the requirement — it never re-searches and never re-runs the closure (the Alethe-pole / R2 trap; `witness-checking survey C2`). Shapes: GuardInPath, FlowNarrowing, QualifierCompatibility, QualifierChain, AssignmentQualifier, DeclarationAttribute (a sub-shaped DU — see Decision 8), DimensionalProduct, CompositionalConstraint, SuppressedByStructure. Plus the **obligation-less verdict witnesses** (Satisfiability/Constructability — invariant 6).

Cross-cutting invariants the checker re-applies **independently** (not trusting the engine). Invariants 1 and 4 are explicitly **cross-cutting over a *family* of witness shapes**, not properties of a single subtype — the recurring under-scope failure (a sound property checked on only one of several discharge paths that exhibit it) is closed by making the checker run these invariants over *every* witness shape in the family:

1. **Sequential staleness (§0.6 item 7) — cross-cutting over all guard-narrowing-AND-relational-narrowing-derived witnesses.** This is the highest-leverage proof-flow invariant and the project's worst failure mode; it is NOT a `GuardInPath`-only property. Narrowing that consults `ReassignedBefore`/`NarrowedValueFromGuard` happens on **five** discharge paths today (a `grep -n 'ReassignedBefore' ProofEngine*.cs` audit returns hits at `Strategies.cs:423,425,678,771,1105-1106`, `QualifierNarrowing.cs:49,142`, `Strategies.cs:97` — each mapped here to a carrying witness), each of which must carry the staleness sets and be re-checked:
   - `GuardInPathWitness` — the numeric/presence/key/index guard path.
   - `FlowNarrowingWitness` — **the relational-narrowing path, the highest-frequency relational cell, and this round's red-team blocker.** `TryFlowNarrowingProof` discharges a field-op-field *relation* (`A − B ≥ 0` via guard/rule `A ≥ B`) and **explicitly gates on staleness of BOTH operand fields**: `if (obligation.ReassignedBefore.Contains(leftField) || obligation.ReassignedBefore.Contains(rightField)) return false;` (`ProofEngine.Strategies.cs:1105-1106`, with the verbatim comment "that relation is stale and must not discharge — for BOTH a guard-sourced and a rule-sourced relation"). Because the narrowing rests on a *relation between two fields*, the `StalenessClean` call for this witness must check **both** `leftField` and `rightField` against `ReassignedBefore` (value-fact kind). A buggy prover that dropped the `ReassignedBefore` check would emit a `Proved` whose witness, if it carried no staleness sets, the checker could not reject — re-opening the *exact* previously-shipped-and-corrected divide-by-zero over-prove on the primary numeric-overflow relational cell. So `FlowNarrowingWitness` carries `StalenessSets` and names its two operand fields (see Decision 2 / the witness shape).
   - `QualifierCompatibilityWitness` — `TryQualifierGuardNarrowingProof` discharges a `QualifierCompatibilityProofRequirement` via `NarrowedValueFromGuard`, which checks `obligation.ReassignedBefore.Contains(field)` (`ProofEngine.QualifierNarrowing.cs:142`); a qualifier-compat `Proved` discharged by a stale guard must be re-rejectable.
   - `AssignmentQualifierWitness` — `TryAssignmentQualifierProof` also falls back to `NarrowedValueFromGuard` (`ProofEngine.QualifierNarrowing.cs:49`).
   - `DeclarationAttributeWitness` (Dimension sub-shape) — the Dimension arm of `TryDeclarationAttributeProof` falls back to `NarrowedPeriodDimensionFromGuard` (`ProofEngine.Strategies.cs:97`), "riding the same all-branches / reassignment-aware narrowing as the qualifier axes."
   Therefore **every witness whose discharge consults `ReassignedBefore` / `NarrowedValueFromGuard` carries the three staleness sets** (verbatim from the obligation, `ProofLedger.cs:34-82`) and/or a re-verifiable narrowing-guard reference, and the checker rejects a cited guard/relation whose field(s) are in `ReassignedBefore` (applying the count-establish/invalidate logic for `count>0` obligations) **on all five**. This re-checks the **exact previously-shipped-and-corrected over-prove** (`spec §0.6:251` — "a guard fact survived a reassignment, proving a divide-by-zero safe") across the whole narrowing family, not just the numeric guard path. (The `ReassignedBefore` grep was re-run across ALL `ProofEngine.*.cs` — not just the qualifier paths — to confirm these five are the complete set and no sixth path exists.)
2. **Composition.** A witness naming a contributing fact whose own obligation is `Unresolved` is rejected (re-confirm each named supporting obligation is itself `Proved`).
3. **Witness composition (Tier-A depends on Tier-B) — and the bare-bounds fallback is NOT a free path; it must be wired.** `IntervalContainmentWitness.Computed` is sound only if `Computed` was soundly derived. **The live discharge reality:** the dispatcher wires *only* `TryIntervalContainmentProofNarrowed` (`ProofEngine.cs:143,1088`), which **always** runs `BuildNarrowedIntervals` (the four-stage composition O-1 is about) even when there is no guard (bare bounds are folded through the same narrowing path); the bare-bounds `TryIntervalContainmentProof` (`Intervals.cs:467`) is **dead — no caller**. So **every** live IntervalContainment `Proved` is narrowing-derived, making this (the highest-frequency numeric-overflow cell, NumericOverflow/78) effectively **O-1-gated wholesale**, not a Tier-A re-execution cell with an optional narrowing reference. Consequently: when `Computed` flows from a narrowing, the Tier-A witness MUST reference the Tier-B `FlowNarrowingWitness` that certified it, and the checker re-verifies the referenced narrowing first. The conservative "re-derive `Computed` from declared field bounds only" fallback (the bare-`ExtractFieldInterval` reader, `Intervals.cs:202`) is the correct *demotion target* if O-1 fails — **but it is not currently a live discharge path; wiring it (re-activating `TryIntervalContainmentProof` or re-deriving bare bounds inside the checker) is required work, not a free fallback.** **Caveat (O-1):** the `FlowNarrowingWitness` is only re-checkable cheaper-than-it-produces if it captures *all* narrowing contributors — see invariant 7 and Decision 2; until O-1 is discharged the soundness theorem does not hold for narrowing-derived intervals, and because the fallback is unwired, a failed O-1 leaves the project's primary overflow cell with no online soundness re-check until the bare path is wired.
4. **Cross-unit cancellation (F-5) — cross-cutting over EVERY unit-cancelling/composing witness shape, keyed on the SOURCE unit strings captured upstream of projection (gated by O-6).** This is the one known-live unsound `Proved` (BUG-030). The cross-unit factor-drop recurs on several structurally distinct paths; verified against the catalog and discharge code:
   - `QualifierCompatibilityProofRequirement` → `TryQualifierCompatibilityProof` first branch → `QualifiersAreCompatible` (same-dimension via `DimensionName` agreement, `ProofEngine.Qualifiers.cs:141-161`). The `Plus/Minus` price ops use this.
   - `QualifierChainProofRequirement` → `TryQualifierCompatibilityProof` **second** branch → `ChainQualifiersMatch` → `ExtractComparableValue`, which for a `Dimension` qualifier returns `DimensionName` (`ProofEngine.Qualifiers.cs:243`). **This is the BUG-030 anchor path:** `OperationKind.PriceTimesQuantity` carries a `QualifierChainProofRequirement` declared on the **Dimension axis for both subjects** (`Operations.cs:649-651`), so `unitPrice in 'USD/kg' × qty in 'g'` matches `'mass' == 'mass'` and drops the kg↔g ×1000 factor.
   - `DimensionalProductProofRequirement` → `TryDimensionalProductProof` → `TryResolveDimensionVector` projects each operand to a `DimensionVector` at *dimension* granularity (`ProofEngine.Qualifiers.cs:62-65`), multiplies, and accepts on a curated-alias match (`:59`). `OperationKind.QuantityTimesQuantity` carries this (`Operations.cs:599-608`); `kg × (1/g)` (same dimension, different unit) cancels to the count alias and drops the factor.
   - `AssignmentQualifierProofRequirement` → `TryAssignmentQualifierProof` value-equality over `ExtractComparableValue` (dimension granularity on the Dimension axis).

   **The hard structural fact this round surfaced (red-team blockers a, c+d):** on the two live anchor paths (`PriceTimesQuantity`/`QuantityTimesQuantity`) the catalog declares the requirement at **dimension granularity**, and the operand unit codes are projected away by `TryProjectUnitToDimension`/`TryProjectCompoundPrice` (`ProofEngine.Qualifiers.cs:944,914`) and by `TryResolveDimensionVector` (`:62-99`) *before* the obligation discharges. By discharge time the qualifier the engine holds is a `DeclaredQualifierMeta.Dimension`, whose only field is `DimensionName` (`DeclaredQualifierMeta.cs`) — it structurally **cannot carry a `UnitCode`**. So a witness constructed at discharge time on these paths carries `mass == mass` with nothing to derive a factor from, and a checker reading only it would *accept* — exactly the false `Proved` BUG-030 is. Two failure modes the naive "witness carries the resolved unit binding" framing falls into: (c) if the engine *hands* the checker a resolved `DeclaredQualifierMeta`, the checker trusts the prover's `ResolveQualifierFromExpression` output verbatim (trusted base secretly depends on the untrusted prover); (d) if the checker *re-derives* the binding, it must re-run `ResolveQualifierFromExpression`/`TryResolveDimensionVector` (the ~900-line resolution engine), blowing the LOC ceiling and re-running the prover. *(Note: the earlier draft named a type `ResolvedQualifier`; no such type exists — the real types are `DeclaredQualifierMeta` and `ResolvedQualifierAxis`. The witness shapes below carry **raw authored unit strings**, not a resolved meta.)*

   **The only prover-free closure (O-6):** the witness must carry the **raw syntactic unit strings as authored** (the `'USD/kg'`, `'g'` source tokens from the field/typed-constant qualifier), captured by `WalkExpression`/the stamping layer at obligation-creation time *before* projection — NOT a resolved meta produced post-resolution. The checker then re-parses those raw strings with **`UcumParser`** (a small, independently-trustable leaf) and computes the conversion factor itself, rejecting a same-dimension-different-unit cancellation unless a UCUM factor is re-derivable. Where the source unit cannot be carried (e.g. the operand is a derived subexpression whose unit only exists post-resolution), that cell is **not prover-free-checkable and must be conservatively rejected (over-rejection), not accepted on a prover-supplied binding.** Therefore **every unit-cancelling/composing witness carries the two raw authored unit strings (`SourceUnitText`), or a flag that the source unit was unrecoverable** (forcing conservative reject): `QualifierCompatibilityWitness`, `QualifierChainWitness`, `DimensionalProductWitness`, `AssignmentQualifierWitness`. **This closure requires capturing the source unit upstream of projection — that capture does not exist today and is the substance of O-6.** Until O-6 is discharged, the cross-unit cell is stated as **not-yet-closed** on the dimension-granularity paths (the design does NOT claim BUG-030 closed as the validating anchor). The cross-unit *policy* (reject / auto-convert / split) remains deferred language surface; this design only makes the factor-drop *checkable once the source unit is reachable*.
5. **Structural suppression (cross-phase) — conditionally accepted, with a cheap consistency stopgap that re-confirms EVERY structural leg the suppression branch actually used.** A `SuppressedByStructureWitness` (for obligations force-`Proved` on transitions from unreachable/dead-end states) references the `ReachabilityFact`/`DeadEndStateFact`(s) (`StateGraph.cs:93,114`) that justified suppression. The proof-engine checker accepts it **only conditionally** — full soundness is parked on the graph reachability verdict being sound, which has no checker until the graph phase ships, **for an indefinite period** (the graph checker is deferred). During that period every suppressed-by-structure `Proved` (one per obligation on every transition from an unreachable/dead-end state, across every stateful precept) rides an unchecked dependency on the untrusted graph analyzer; the plan must **count this fraction across the sample corpus** and state it in the soundness theorem as trusted-not-checked-until-graph-checker-ships. **The witness shape distinguishes the two suppression branches because they rest on a different NUMBER of structural facts** (verified against `ProofEngine.cs:1176` vs `:1187-1189`):
   - **Unreachable-`FromState` branch** (`:1176`, `unreachableStates.Contains(fromState)`) rests on a **single** `ReachabilityFact` naming the `FromState` — one leg.
   - **Dead-end-to-dead-end branch** (`:1187-1189`, `deadEndStates.Contains(fromState) && successRow.TargetState is not null && deadEndStates.Contains(successRow.TargetState)`) rests on **two** `DeadEndStateFact`s — one for the `FromState`, one for the transition's `TargetState` — two legs.
   So the witness is a two-variant shape: `SuppressedByStructureWitness.Unreachable(ReachabilityFact FromStateReachability)` and `SuppressedByStructureWitness.DeadEndToDeadEnd(DeadEndStateFact FromStateDeadEnd, DeadEndStateFact TargetStateDeadEnd)`. **Stopgap (the conditional arm's actual, limited guarantee):** even without re-verifying reachability, the suppression-pass checker arm re-confirms that **every cited structural fact actually exists in `ProducedFacts` and names the precondition it was used for** — for the unreachable branch, the `ReachabilityFact` exists and names the obligation's `FromState`; **for the dead-end branch, BOTH `DeadEndStateFact`s exist, one naming the obligation's `FromState` and one naming the transition's `TargetState`.** A single-leg stopgap (re-confirming only the `FromState`) would accept a fabricated/misapplied dead-end witness whose `FromState` is genuinely dead-end but whose `TargetState` is NOT — the soundness-relevant `TargetState`-dead-end precondition the branch at `:1189` actually required. So the stopgap verifies *exactly the precondition each branch used* — one leg for unreachable, two legs for dead-end — catching a fabricated witness that cites a non-produced fact, the wrong state, or (for dead-end) a real `FromState` fact with a missing/wrong `TargetState` fact. **The suppression post-pass (`IncorporateForwardingFacts`, `ProofEngine.cs:1166-1197`, with its two direct `Strategy = ProofStrategy.Literal` writes at `:1181`/`:1194`) must be rewired to construct the correct witness variant** rather than overloading `Strategy = ProofStrategy.Literal` (Decision 7): a force-`Proved` masquerading as a `LiteralWitness` would be *rejected* by the Tier-A literal re-execution (its site is a transition row, not a literal), causing mass over-rejection on every unreachable-state transition; a force-`Proved` with a null witness violates the "every `Proved` carries a witness" invariant. (Verified: only `ReachabilityFact`/`DeadEndStateFact` drive suppression here; `UnreachableRowFact` (`ProofEngine.Satisfiability.cs:190`) is *produced* but NOT consumed for suppression, so this invariant's scoping to the graph reachability verdict is correct.)
6. **Obligation-less proof-engine verdicts (Satisfiability / Constructability) — REJECT-direction re-confirmable; clean-on-`null` and the friction-direction scans carved out.** Several whole-construct verdicts are emitted directly with no obligation site (`ProofEngine.Satisfiability.cs` header: "whole-construct ... with no obligation site"). This round's red-team and completeness-critic showed the naive "the checker re-confirms the clean verdict" framing is *unsound as stated*, so the invariant is split by direction and by which scans are clean-direction soundness-critical:
   - **(a) default-satisfiability / global-rule-fold (`ScanRulesAgainstDefaults`, `ProofEngine.Satisfiability.cs:79`) and (c) `CheckInitialStateSatisfiability` (constructability) are the only two CLEAN-direction soundness-critical scans** — a false clean lets an entity be constructed into a rule-violating / unconstructable configuration (Principle-1 breach, `soundness-case §1.1`). **But the scan is prove-or-reject ONLY on `is false`**: a rule is reported "clean" when `ConstantFold` returns `true` OR `null` (unfoldable — a computed-field default, a non-constant default, any relational rule), and the **dominant clean case is `null`** (verified: `ProofEngine.Satisfiability.cs:79` rejects only on `is false`). A checker re-evaluating `ConstantFold` to `null` learns nothing — it cannot distinguish "genuinely satisfied" from "unverifiable" — and to even reach `null`/`false` it would have to reconstruct `BuildDefaultEnvironment` (`:69`), pulling the default-environment builder into the trusted base. **Therefore the `SatisfiabilityWitness`/`ConstructabilityWitness` checker can only re-confirm the REJECT direction** (re-derive the `is false` fold the scan relied on, catching a fabricated witness whose carried fold actually folds false), and **the `null`/clean direction is explicitly carved OUT of the §2 soundness theorem** as a stated boundary (like the suppression cell), NOT claimed brought-in. Closing the clean direction would require carrying the full folded environment + per-leaf fold trace so the checker re-derives true/false/unfoldable from scratch with its own `ConstantFold` *and* its own `BuildDefaultEnvironment` — a real trusted-base expansion (sized in O-7) that the ~900 LOC ceiling does not currently admit. The honest disposition is the carve-out; the reject-direction re-check (a hollow-witness defense for the cases the scan *does* reject) is the delivered guarantee.
   - **(b) `UnsatisfiableGuard`/`TautologicalGuard`/`ContradictoryRule`/`VacuousRule` scans are FRICTION-DIRECTION, not clean-direction soundness-critical, and are REMOVED from the "brought into the channel" claim.** Their CLEAN verdict is not soundness-critical: a false-clean `UnsatisfiableGuard`/`TautologicalGuard`/`ContradictoryRule` does not make a faulting or unconstructable entity compile (these scans only *raise friction diagnostics*; failing to raise one is an over-acceptance of a benign program, not a fault). They also use a *different* mechanism (`IsGuardUnsatisfiableUnderFieldBounds`/`IsPredicateProvablyTrue` per-field `NumericInterval` emptiness/tautology, `ProofEngine.Satisfiability.cs:172-235`) whose evidence the `RuleFolds`/`FoldedDefaults` shape cannot represent. Only `DefaultViolatesRule` and `UnsatisfiableInitialState` are clean-direction soundness-critical (and per the bullet above, only in the reject direction). So these four scans are *not* in the witness channel and *not* in the soundness theorem.
   (The CompositionalConstraint blocked-self-unsat sub-case in Decision 4 is a *distinct, narrower* obligation-bearing path.)

- **Principle 7 (totality) & 11 (static completeness)** continue to hold *more strongly* after this construct ships **for every verdict family the checker covers** — with **four stated boundaries** the soundness theorem does not yet close (and does not silently claim): (i) the **relational narrowing tier** (`FlowNarrowing`/narrowing-derived `IntervalContainment`) is sound under the checker only once Open Question **O-1** is discharged (the `FlowNarrowingWitness` must capture *all* narrowing contributors, not just cited field-op-field relations; until then narrowing-derived intervals fall back to bare-bounds re-derivation and the theorem is stated as not-yet-holding for that cell — see Decision 2 / invariants 3, 7). The checker introduces no new expression form whose evaluation is undefined; it is a pure predicate over existing data.

**Coverage-theorem residuals (enumerated in one place).** The §3 coverage half is complete at five structural levels — emission-site presence, procedural-synthesizer firing, walk-arm presence-plus-stamp-firing, **child-position recursion completeness** (Decision 5 / the `TypedMemberAccess.Arguments` fix), and **construct-position entry-point completeness** (Decision 5 / the guard-walking disposition). ## Architecture Grounding

### Precept-internal placement

**Layer placement.** Three pieces, three layers:
- The **witness data-model** (`ProofWitness` DU) is *catalog-adjacent language metadata* — it lives beside `ProofRequirement`/`ProofSatisfaction` (`src/Precept/Language/`), mirroring the existing `[CatalogDU]` shape-as-identity convention (`ProofRequirement.cs:41`).
- The **checker** (`WitnessChecker`) is a *pipeline stage* — a pure post-Pass-2 pass inside the proof engine, reading the catalogs as its oracle. It belongs in pipeline code (not catalog metadata) because it is a *procedure* (re-verification), but it pulls all its ground truth from catalog metadata (`Operators`/`Types`/`Modifiers`/`Functions`/`DimensionCatalog`), never a parallel rule list — the CLAUDE.md catalog non-negotiable *is* its trusted base.
- The **coverage analyzers** are *Roslyn analyzers* (`src/Precept.Analyzers/`) — they enforce a build-time invariant about the *pipeline's own source*, exactly the layer PRECEPT0019/0027 already occupy.

**Cross-component propagation:**
- **Runtime (parser, type checker, evaluator, diagnostics):** the `Try*` discharge functions change return type `bool → ProofWitness?` and the `TryDischarge` dispatcher (`ProofEngine.cs:1054`) propagates the witness; `ProofObligation` gains a `Witness` field; a new `WitnessChecker` pass runs after discharge. The type checker is unaffected except that its stamping classes gain coverage annotations. The evaluator is untouched.
- **Tooling (syntax highlighting, completions, hover, semantic tokens):** LS hover that today reads `obligation.Strategy`/`ComputedInterval` reads the structured `Witness` instead (richer, but the same data — the projections preserve the old fields). No grammar/completion/token impact.
- **MCP (vocabulary, DTOs, tool output):** `precept_proofs` gains the structured witness in its output DTO (the §0.6 #13 consumer named in the spec). No new MCP *tool*; a DTO field addition.

**Breaking changes.** No public *language* surface changes. Internal: `ProofObligation`'s shape changes (a field added; `Strategy`/`ComputedInterval` become witness projections) — this touches LS hover, MCP `precept_proofs`, and `EdgeProofStatus` consumers that read those fields. No diagnostic codes change for authors; the new `PRECEPT00NN` coverage analyzer codes are *pipeline-source* diagnostics (build errors for Precept maintainers), not author-facing PRE codes.

### External architectural precedent

**Verasco** (Jourdan, Laporte, Blazy, Leroy, Pichardie — *A Formally-Verified C Static Analyzer*, POPL 2015), § 6.3, on validating the convex-polyhedra domain a-posteriori rather than verifying the search:

> "The verified validator approach is a perfect match for the polyhedral domain... the operations over polyhedra (inclusion test, convex hull, projection) are implemented by an untrusted, efficient OCaml library, which produces certificates that are checked by verified Coq code. For example, the inclusion test P₁ ⊆ P₂ produces, for each constraint of P₂, a certificate expressing it as a nonnegative linear combination of the constraints of P₁ (a Farkas certificate); the verified checker only needs to verify the linear combination."

Precept takes Verasco's **verify-simple/validate-hard split** wholesale: the entire ~7.8 KLOC discharge engine (`ProofEngine.*.cs`, `wc -l` = 7815) is the untrusted "efficient library"; a small trusted checker re-verifies a certificate per `Proved`. Precept **diverges** in two ways grounded in spec §0.6 philosophy #3 ("opaque solvers rejected on principle") and §0.4 (no fixpoint/widening): (1) Precept's certificates must be *legible structured data*, not a solver trace — so the witness is a named-DU, re-checkable by humans/agents (LS hover, MCP), not just by a verified kernel; (2) Precept does not need Farkas certificates for the *current* 11 strategies (they are interval/named-guard re-checks, not polyhedral joins) — the Farkas-shaped certificate slot is reserved for the deferred Karr work, exactly the headroom the trusted-base sizing reserves. The granularity choice (fine-grained per-obligation certificate, DRAT-pole, not a coarse strategy-name tag, Alethe-pole) follows DRAT/LRAT (Wetzler/Heule/Hunt, SAT 2014): a fine-grained certificate is cheaply re-checkable; a coarse one forces the checker to re-derive.

## Decisions

### Decision 1: Carry a fine-grained `ProofWitness` DU on every `Proved` obligation, keyed by witness shape (~14-15), realizing spec §0.6 #13.

**Stakes**: high

- **Rationale**: §0.6 #13 + Proof-philosophy #6 already *mandate* structured proof attribution; today `ProofObligation` carries only `Strategy` + `ComputedInterval` + the staleness sets, not the contributing-fact chain. The witness completes an existing locked spec mandate, structured as re-checkable data. Keying by *shape* (not the 11-member `ProofStrategy` enum) is forced because `GuardInPath` discharges Numeric/Presence/KeyPresence/IndexBounds via structurally different witness data and `QualifierCompatibility` covers compat/chain/assignment — keying by enum would force the checker to re-discriminate sub-cases at check time, re-introducing prover logic into the trusted base (the Alethe coarse-witness trap).
- **Tradeoff accepted**: a witness shape can no longer be inferred from the `Strategy` tag alone; the discharge code that previously set only a tag must now build a structured witness, and the `Strategy↔shape` map is many-to-many.
- **Alternatives considered**: (a) one shape per `ProofStrategy` enum member — rejected: collapses distinct certificates, forces checker re-discrimination, bloats the trusted base. (b) one uniform certificate for all strategies — rejected: over-engineers Tier A (a certificate for `5 ∈ [0,10]` is pure overhead) and risks the coarse-granularity trap. (c) a side-channel attribution map keyed by obligation — rejected: forks a parallel structure from the record the witness naturally rides; `ProofForwardingFact` (`StateGraph.cs:91`) already establishes the on-record DU convention.
- **Precedent**: `ProofForwardingFact` on-record DU (`StateGraph.cs:91-139`); `ProofRequirement` `[CatalogDU]` shape-as-identity (`ProofRequirement.cs:41`); `ProofSatisfaction` positive-carrier DU (`ProofRequirement.cs:382-414`); DRAT/LRAT fine-grained certificates.

**Stakes**: high
