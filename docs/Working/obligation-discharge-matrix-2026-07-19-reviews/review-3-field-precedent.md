## 1. Where has this shape been done?

**Event-B/Rodin — the closest analogue, and it substantially validates the shape.** The repo's own comparator research already establishes the structural identity: "A Precept definition is an Event-B abstract machine in .NET DSL form... The mapping is exact" (`research/philosophy/formal-spec-languages-comparators.md:164`) and "Event-B generates a proof obligation for each event: given that the invariants hold before the event fires, prove they hold after" (same file, §4 item 1). What that research file does not detail (training-data grounded, flagged as such): Event-B's semantics is *normatively defined through its proof-obligation taxonomy* — named obligation kinds (INV invariant-preservation, FIS feasibility, GRD guard-strengthening, WD well-definedness), generated rule-per-construct, each labeled `event/invariant/KIND`. Abrial's book defines the PO-generation rules as part of the method's definition; Rodin's Proof Obligation Generator mechanizes them. So "obligation kinds × discharge, enumerated per construct, as the definition of what the checker must do" is not novel — it is the load-bearing definitional mechanism of a 25-year production formal method (Paris Métro lines 14/1, per the same research file §2.3). **The transfer lesson**: Rodin avoids drift because obligations are *generated from the model by rules*, not hand-enumerated in a document. Precept's matrix is hand-enumerated prose; only the obligation-family axis is catalog-backed (doc line 29 cites `ProofRequirement` records); the rule-structure and write-structure axes (line 125) are not. See F3.

**Dafny/SPARK WP-based VC generation.** The doc's obligation column — "weakest precondition of the rule through the write" (lines 15, 60) — is textbook Dijkstra WP calculus, the exact mechanism of Dafny→Boogie and SPARK VC-gen (surveyed in-repo: `research/architecture/compiler/type-proof-stage-contract-survey.md`). The field lesson from that lineage: the VC generator, not the prover, is where the language's proof semantics actually lives — which supports the doc's move of making the minted obligation an explicit, documented column rather than an implementation detail.

**Conformance-suite-as-definition — the failure modes are known and mostly addressed, with one hazard live.** The in-repo survey (`research/architecture/spec-conformance-test-suite-survey.md`) is directly on point:

- *Under-specification / silent cells*: the field's answer is a visible denominator — ACATS Coverage Documents, Kotlin's green/gray per-sentence coloring (survey C2, lines 92–96). The matrix's "a cell with no answer is a documentation gap by construction" (doc line 8) and the edge-cell disposition table (lines 111–121) are precisely this practice, done well.
- *Oracle drift toward the implementation*: the survey's C4 (lines 104–108) — blessing/baselining shifts the oracle to "what the implementation emits." The matrix resists this correctly: expected outcomes are stated by the definition before the run (doc line 137), and the ✓v provenance marks (line 42) keep "model-derived" and "verified-live" distinct rather than blessing live behavior as the definition. Good.
- *Combinatorial explosion*: survey C5 — the field prunes via category-partition equivalence classes, whose known blind spot is interactions *between* classes ("equivalence-class partitioning can miss interaction bugs that lie between classes," survey line 114). The doc's pruned four-axis product with per-family arity-equivalence judgments (line 125) is exactly category-partition — including its blind spot. See F5.
- *WASM's actual structure*: the suite is co-located with a formal semantics and a reference interpreter — the suite *accompanies* the definition, it is not the sole definition (survey line 49). The matrix doc gets this right by declaring itself substrate for the canonical-doc distillation (line 133), not the canonical artifact.

**Does the small surface neutralize the classic failure modes?** Substantially, yes — and this is the genuinely novel part. Every comparator's hardest problem is that its spec denominator is prose or another implementation; the conformance survey's top Open Question (line 124): "No comparator has a machine-readable spec like Precept's catalogs... whether a catalog-as-denominator approach is a genuine novelty or a known anti-pattern is unresolved — the survey found no precedent either way." A loop-free, function-free, non-Turing-complete surface with catalog-enumerated obligation families makes "exhaustive over the pruned matrix" *plausibly finite* in a way no surveyed comparator achieved — ACATS says outright exhaustiveness is unattainable for Ada (survey line 51). But the small surface does **not** neutralize the two failure modes that live in judgment rather than in the input space: the equivalence-class collapse (F5) and hand-maintained axes drifting from the catalogs (F3).

## 2. The certificate claim — real or cosmetic?

**Real at the schema level; overstated at the granularity level.** The schema correspondence holds: obligation↔theorem, premises↔premises, derivation↔derivation matches the want doc's certificate spec verbatim ("per obligation, the theorem, the premises used... and the derivation," want doc line 199). And sharing vocabulary between definition-artifact and certificate is well-precedented: in LF-style systems the definition (inference rules) and the certificates (proof terms) literally share one signature; in Event-B the PO names index both the definitional obligation and Rodin's per-PO discharge status; in PCC the safety policy/VC generator is the shared artifact between producer and consumer (in-repo grounding: `witness-checking-soundness-architecture-survey.md`, PCC/LCF section, lines 92–96).

The problem is line 18's "each matrix row is a certificate entry in the making." The cell's derivation column is a *strategy name plus proof class* ("§1a guard-match", "`IntervalContainment`" — doc lines 66–67). The want doc's certificate is a *step-by-step walkable derivation*: "The certificate records the finished proof step by step, and the checker simply walks those steps and confirms each one" (want doc line 206). The in-repo witness-checking survey makes exactly this distinction load-bearing, with primary evidence: fine-grained DRAT-style certificates give cheap checking and a small kernel; coarse Alethe-style certificates ("by linear arithmetic") force the checker to re-derive — "a witness that merely names the strategy ('proved by IntervalContainment') is coarse-grained and useless for checking" (survey line 120; C1 at lines 160–164). A matrix cell is, in that survey's terms, an Alethe-granularity record. That is *fine for a definition document* — but if the "same triple" framing hardens into "the matrix row schema is the certificate format," Precept walks into the documented Alethe trap. Benefit and risk are both real; the fix is one sentence of scoping.

## 3. The negative-space claim — completeness of premise-withheld enumeration

The pair structure tests two behaviors: reject-when-premise-absent and accept-when-named-premise-present. The field says this covers one mutant class and misses the most severe failure direction:

- **Wrong-strength premises (near-misses) are invisible to the enumeration.** A base plus `Amount1 max 600, Amount2 max 600` (sum 1200 > 1000) contains a premise of the right *class* and right *shape* that must still fail to discharge — no cell in the base-first structure ever exercises it. This is the *unsound acceptance* direction, and for a prove-or-reject engine it is the worst failure class: the want doc's own bar is "a compiler that accepts any of these mutations is not the compiler this document asks for" (want doc line 131) — but its mutation table, and this matrix, enumerate only absence-mutations. Field practice is explicit that both verdict polarities must be first-class: SV-COMP benchmark tasks carry expected verdicts in both directions and penalize a wrong "correct" (false proof) far more heavily than a wrong "incorrect"; ACATS Class B grading requires the invalid construct rejected *and* nothing spurious (conformance survey line 51). The soundness-bug literature confirms deletion-style negatives don't find prover unsoundness: differential testing of production analyzers (Klinger, Christakis, Wüstholz, ISSTA 2019), Amazon's Dafny fuzzing experience paper, and blackbox mutational fuzzing of SMT solvers all found soundness bugs via *generated and perturbed inputs*, not premise deletion from known-good programs.
- **Wrong-reason rejection can vacuously pass the reject half.** ACATS Class B: "each indicated error in the test is detected and reported, and no other errors are reported" (conformance survey line 51); survey C3: assert the specific diagnostic at the specific stage. Doc line 22 requires the specific obligation named — good — but does not require *absence of other* diagnostics, so a base that rejects for an incidental reason (typo-level error) satisfies the cell as written.
- **Redundant-premise masking** (deleting one premise while another silently closes the proof) is the deletion method's classic blind spot; the doc's addition framing on *verified-minimal* bases (✓v, line 42) largely mitigates it — worth stating base-minimality as a required discipline rather than an incidental practice.

## Findings

**F1 — blocking. The matrix structure has no cell for "must NOT discharge."** The unsound-acceptance direction — wrong-strength or wrong-shape premises that look like discharges — is unrepresentable in the current pair definition, yet it is the failure class the field finds most dangerous and hardest to catch (SV-COMP asymmetric scoring; Klinger et al. ISSTA 2019; ACATS Class B). *Smallest amendment*: extend each defined cell from a behavior *pair* to a behavior *triple* — add a required **near-miss row** per discharge addition (the same addition with a bound weakened past sufficiency, or a guard on the wrong condition) whose required outcome is: still rejects, same obligation named. This is mechanical from the existing rows (perturb the constant that makes the arithmetic close) and preserves "enumerable by construction."

**F2 — weakens. "Each matrix row is a certificate entry in the making" (line 18) overstates the correspondence at the granularity that the repo's own research says matters most.** The cell's derivation column is a strategy name (Alethe-pole); the want doc's certificate is a step-by-step walkable record (DRAT-pole; want doc line 206; `witness-checking-soundness-architecture-survey.md` C1). *Smallest amendment*: one sentence in the triple paragraph — the cell triple is the certificate's *schema and index* (same theorem, same premise list, same strategy taxonomy); the certificate additionally carries the fine-grained derivation the cell only names, and the matrix must never be read as fixing the certificate's payload granularity.

**F3 — weakens. Only one of the four enumeration axes is catalog-anchored.** Line 29 grounds obligation family in `ProofRequirement` records; rule structure and write structure (line 125) are prose taxonomies with no named catalog source. The field's drift lesson (Test262 abandoning section-numbers for stable `esid` anchors, Kotlin's relinker — conformance survey C1) and Rodin's generate-from-model discipline both say hand-maintained enumeration axes rot. Precept is uniquely positioned to avoid this (catalog-before-code is already the repo rule). *Smallest amendment*: per axis, name the catalog/source that enumerates it, or explicitly mark the axis "prose taxonomy — catalog anchor pending," so the gap is a written statement (the doc's own discipline, line 121).

**F4 — minor. Adopt ACATS-grade outcome assertions on both halves.** Reject half: the named obligation *and no other diagnostics* (rules out wrong-reason rejection). Discharge half: line 23's "clean compile" already covers it. Also promote base-minimality (base rejects *only* for the target obligation) from incidental ✓v practice to a stated per-cell requirement.

**F5 — minor. The exhaustiveness claim inherits category-partition's between-class blind spot.** Line 125 already requires the arity-equivalence judgment be "recorded, not assumed" — good, and better than most of the field. Strengthen it one notch: the recorded judgment should state *why* arity doesn't change the derivation for that family (the justification, not just the verdict), because the conformance survey's C5 tradeoff (interaction bugs between equivalence classes) is precisely where a pruned matrix's "exhaustive" claim fails in practice.

## Verdict

**Novel-but-defensible, leaning sound — this is not an anti-pattern.** The core shape has strong partial precedent on each leg: obligation-taxonomy-as-definition is Event-B/Rodin's production-proven definitional mechanism; WP-minted obligations are the Dafny/SPARK lineage; no-silent-cells with dispositions is Kotlin/ACATS denominator practice; the certificate schema-sharing has LF/PCC/Event-B precedent. The genuinely novel part — treating the matrix as *exhaustive by construction* over a finite catalog-backed surface — is exactly the opportunity the repo's own conformance survey flagged as unprecedented-either-way, and Precept's loop-free non-Turing-complete surface is the one setting where the field's "exhaustiveness is unattainable" humility (ACATS) may genuinely not apply. The two field practices to import now, while it is a draft: **(1)** the SV-COMP/ACATS both-polarities discipline — near-miss must-not-discharge rows per cell (F1, the only blocking finding); **(2)** the DRAT/Alethe granularity scoping on the certificate claim (F2). Both are one-paragraph-scale amendments that close the two places where the field has documented scar tissue.
