## 1. The population pass

### Cell count, derived from the doc's own axes (working shown)

The doc's case shapes are a DU of axis sets, not one product. Taking each shape's axes at the doc's own enumerations:

- **Rule families at authored write sites**: 2 families (establishment, preservation) × 6 rule structures × 4 authored write-site categories (handler `set`, entry hook/state action, collection action, computed-field transitive; editable is its own shape) × 16 RHS read-set combinations (subsets of {written field, args, other mentioned fields, unmentioned fields}, empty set = constant) × 4 type families = **3,072**
- **No-write rows** (no RHS axis; three kinds per review-1 F-8): 2 × 6 × 3 × 4 = **144**
- **Fault family**: 13 operation kinds (`ProofRequirementKind`, measured) × ~6 evaluation-site categories × 4 type families ≈ **312**
- **Editable-ingress**: 6 rule structures × 4 types ≈ **24–48**
- **Structural**: 5 `ProofForwardingFact` kinds × ~3 state/row shapes ≈ **15**

**Disposition product ≈ 3,500–3,600 cells** (robust range 3,000–5,500 depending on how the evaluation-site and no-write sub-axes are keyed). This is the same order as the 4,592-cell gap-analysis corpus — which in-repo precedent shows agents enumerated, deduped, and disposition-accounted in roughly two days (STATUS.md Phase 0/1).

Of these, the doc's own gates prune most to one-liners: the quantified and temporal rule-structure rows (2 of 6) are **open** by citation (⧖ Q10, Q14a) — ~1,000 cells at one line each; a large share of the read-set × write-site × type crossings are **empty** (e.g., collection-action × single-field-primitive). The empty-cell "written derivation" requirement is real but formulaic: the derivations instantiate a few dozen pruning rules, generated by agents and presence-checked by the validator. **Fully-defined cells** — the ones needing schema + contracts + suggestion schemas + witness triple + near-miss + respellability + citations — I estimate at **300–650**: 4 live rule structures × 4 write sites × 4 types = 64 contract families, times ~3–6 materially distinct read-set classes each (the doc's own Witness Family 1 shows 3 per rule), plus 60–120 fault cells (catalog-settled, ✓v-capable today) and ~25 editable/structural. This is my estimate; the doc's schema-class definition ("two programs sit in the same cell iff their obligations unify with the same schema") is what keeps it bounded.

### Per-cell effort and where it strains

Witness Family 1 produced 3 fully-defined, live-verified cells in one drafting pass. With the WP calculator mechanizing the schema and suggestion-schema templates shared per premise class (there are only ~5 classes), I estimate 30–90 minutes of agent time per defined cell including a validator pass — **300–650 agent-hours ≈ a few weeks of parallel agent runs**, with owner effort bounded by the ratification protocol (generative sections read in full — a few pages — plus spot-checks). Near-miss rows are mechanical (weaken the bound past the arithmetic).

The burden strains in exactly one place: **the respellability verdict**. It is a universally-quantified semantic claim over an infinite band ("*every* sound program in the band has a licensed respelling"), per cell, not mechanizable, and — per review-4 F6, which the doc has not yet absorbed as a requirement — currently asserted, never measured, while being load-bearing for surface-shrink decisions. The structure invites the fix: verdicts stated once per contract family with per-cell inheritance, plus the F6 corpus pass (adjudicate the 59 rule-bearing samples against the contracts before ratification). That converts the weakest plank from hundreds of hand-asserted universals into one measured claim per family. Other descope levers already latent in the structure: suggestion schemas as shared class templates, and a "type-uniform" annotation collapsing type-family cells where the contract doesn't vary (primitive vs business-domain numeric are near-identical in Family 1's reasoning).

## 3. The compiler obligations the matrix imposes (re-verified, not inherited)

The prior feasibility conclusion (`feasibility.md`: yes / yes / no-new-theory, all headline claims adversarially confirmed per DECISION-PACKET §(a)2) predates three rulings. Re-assessment:

**(a) Exact-iff.** The "never more" half costs almost nothing to implement — but it imposes a real, previously-unpriced **conformance audit**: the shipped engine's actual accepted set (13 requirement kinds × strategies) must be brought into exact correspondence with the populated contracts, narrowing any incidental generality or widening the contracts definition-first. Bounded (the strategy surface is enumerable, `ProofStrategy` at `ProofLedger.cs:100`) but real — call it a medium slice. The "every licensed derivation" half is free **iff every contract names a decision procedure** — which the matrix's own normal-form-equal discipline mostly guarantees, but review-4 F3's finding stands: the reject side of iff is structurally uncertifiable (no certificate of non-derivability), so the cell→test matrix becomes the *sole* verification mechanism for half the contract. That raises the test-coverage bar; it does not add machinery. No new proof theory either way.

**(b) Near-miss behavior.** Near-free. The engine is already obligation-centric: a failed discharge leaves the obligation Unresolved and the diagnostic names it (that is precisely how mutation 6 behaves today — PRE0083 flips Proved→Unresolved on the same obligation). The requirement is stable obligation identity plus a test shape, not new machinery.

**(c) Suggestion schemas, all applicable classes.** The prior analysis priced missing-premise computation as textbook backward substitution (#10), already demanded by the want. The matrix's delta — computing the applicable-class set per cell (derivable from read-set ∩ mention set, ingredients already in `GraphAnalyzer.FieldWriteSites.cs` and `ConstraintInfluenceEntry.ReferencedFields`) and rendering one schema per class rather than one suggestion — is a modest increment, ~20–30% on the diagnostics slice. The WP is computed once and shared with (a)'s calculator.

**Deferred/new machinery check**: the matrix keeps §1b explicitly Deferred (edge cell 1) and stays inside the prior analysis's classification. Genuinely new items: the WP calculator (small, shared infrastructure), the conformance audit (medium), definition-version stamps riding in certificates (small bookkeeping on #8). **The prior yes/yes/no verdict survives**, with the build materially reshaped in one way: the exact-iff ruling converts "completeness relative to the licensed system" from aspiration to conformance, which is mostly a bigger, mechanically-generated test matrix plus regression discipline — the cells themselves generate it.

## Verdict

**Feasible as specified** — with the explicit understanding that "as specified" includes the pre-population deliverables the doc itself already mandates (ratification protocol, day-one validator, WP calculator), and with two cheap restructurings strongly advised, both already latent in the reviews: (1) respellability verdicts stated per contract family and grounded in the 59-sample corpus measurement before ratification, rather than hand-asserted per cell; (2) every discharge contract names its decision procedure, making the iff's completeness half free by construction. Neither changes the matrix's structure; both close its weakest planks. Nothing in the program is research-grade, nothing requires machinery the prior analysis classified as deferred (§1b stays deferred by the matrix's own edge cell), and every sizing here has an in-repo measured precedent within the same order of magnitude.

## The three biggest risks, in rank order

1. **Model-derived population at scale before the machinery can verify it.** Most cells cannot carry ✓v until rule-obligation minting exists; this is the exact failure class of the 07-14 probe (447 untrustworthy verdicts), and respellability verdicts are its sharpest instance — universally-quantified, unmeasured, and load-bearing for surface decisions. Mitigations exist (WP calculator, validator, verdict-from-codes discipline, the F6 corpus pass) but must actually be enforced as ratification gates, not conventions.
2. **The exact-iff conformance burden on the shipped engine.** The reject side of iff is structurally test-only-verifiable, and the existing engine's accepted set must be audited into exact correspondence with the populated contracts — narrowing incidental generality or widening contracts definition-first. Bounded but unpriced by the prior analysis; a silent under-proving regression passes every certificate check and is caught only by cell-generated tests, which makes test-generation completeness load-bearing.
3. **Owner decision throughput on the ⧖ gates.** Q1 and S4 determine which cells *exist*; Q5, Q6, class-(e), and the normal form determine large regions' content. Each is cheap to rule (the packet frames them as one-sentence decisions) but expensive to leave open — the population pass can absorb roughly two to three weeks of unblocked work before the gated regions become the frontier.
